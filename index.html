About this data

Programs. 13 programs where DDF and at least one other funder put money into the same fund or grant program. Colors follow DDF's program names on dorisduke.org (Arts, Environment, Medical Research, Child Well-being, Building Bridges). Humanity AI is not housed in one DDF program, so it is shown as "Other". No pooled program was found for Building Bridges. Gray dots are funders that share programs across more than one DDF program. DDF was called the Doris Duke Charitable Foundation before 2023, and older sources use that name.

Endowments. Dot size shows total assets (end-of-year book value) from each funder's latest IRS Form 990 or 990-PF on ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, with the fiscal year shown in the info box. Book value can differ from the market value a foundation reports. Funders with no figure (public charities, LLCs, smaller family foundations) are drawn as small dashed rings.

Confidence (●●●● 4/4). Every program and endowment figure was checked on 26 Nov 2026. 4 means it matches a primary source exactly (the funder's own release or page, or an IRS filing). 3 means a primary source with a small gap, which the note names (for example a missing end year, a list that says "including", or a figure the source states inconsistently). 2 means a secondary source such as Wikipedia or a news report. 1 means unverified.

Line width. DDF rarely publishes its own share, so lines use each program's total pool (log scale). Dashed lines mean the pool size is not published. The Mosaic Fund shows 11 of its 27 donors.

Filled and hollow dots. Each dot represents a funder. A filled dot means a public endowment figure was found, and its size shows that endowment. A hollow dashed dot means no reliable endowment figure was found, so it is drawn at a fixed size.
